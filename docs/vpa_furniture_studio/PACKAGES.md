## 🎯 Choosing the Right Package

### Ask Yourself:

**1. Do you manufacture furniture or just sell it?**
- **Just Sell:** → Starter Package
- **Manufacture:** → Professional or higher

**2. How many units do you produce monthly?**
- **< 50 units:** → Professional
- **50-200 units:** → Advanced
- **200+ units:** → Enterprise or Ultimate

**3. Do you have barcode scanners or plan to implement them?**
- **No:** → Starter or Professional
- **Yes:** → Advanced or higher

**4. Do you need quality certifications (ISO, etc.)?**
- **No:** → Any package except Enterprise
- **Yes:** → Enterprise or Ultimate

**5. Do you sell online (B2C)?**
- **No:** → Any package except Ultimate
- **Yes:** → Ultimate

**6. What's your annual revenue?**
- **< $500k:** → Starter
- **$500k - $2M:** → Professional
- **$2M - $5M:** → Advanced
- **$5M - $20M:** → Enterprise
- **$20M+:** → Ultimate
