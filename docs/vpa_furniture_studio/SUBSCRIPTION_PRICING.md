## 💡 Which Option Should You Choose?

### Choose **Monthly** if:
- You want to try the system with minimal commitment
- Cash flow is tight and you need low monthly payments
- You're unsure about long-term needs
- You want flexibility to scale up/down

### Choose **Yearly** if:
- You're committed to using the system for at least 1 year
- You want to save 17% vs. monthly pricing
- You need better support SLAs
- You want access to training and webinars

### Choose **One-Time** if:
- You have capital budget available
- You want lowest total cost of ownership (2+ years)
- You prefer to own software perpetually
- You have internal IT for maintenance
- You want full control and customization freedom

## 🔄 Subscription Terms

### What Happens When Subscription Ends?

**Monthly/Yearly Subscriptions:**
- Software **stops working** 30 days after last payment
- Your data remains accessible (read-only mode)
- You can export all data for 90 days
- Reactivate anytime to restore full functionality

**One-Time Purchase:**
- Software **works forever**
- You keep the version you purchased
- Support/updates require separate annual contract (20% of license price)
- Upgrade to new versions at 50% discount

### Migration Options:
- **Subscription → One-Time:** Apply up to 12 months of subscription payments toward purchase price
- **Monthly → Yearly:** Switch anytime, credit unused monthly balance
- **Upgrade Package:** Pay difference only (pro-rated for subscriptions)
- **Downgrade Package:** Credit applied to future months (no refunds)
