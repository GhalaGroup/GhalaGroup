📊 Module 5: vpa_furniture_reports

Comprehensive Reporting & Business Intelligence

📦 Phase: Phase 1 (Months 1-6) 🏷️ Type: Costing & Reporting ⚡ Priority: Medium $3,000

📋 Overview

The vpa_furniture_reports module provides comprehensive reporting and business intelligence dashboards specifically designed for furniture manufacturing businesses.

📈 Executive Dashboard

KPI overview with real-time metrics

💼 Sales Analytics

Order pipeline, conversion rates, top customers

🏭 Production Reports

Manufacturing efficiency and capacity utilization

📦 Inventory Reports

Stock levels, turnover, reorder alerts

🎯 Available Reports

1. Executive Dashboard

  • Revenue This Month: Total sales vs. target
  • Orders in Progress: Active manufacturing orders
  • Average Profit Margin: Company-wide profitability
  • On-Time Delivery Rate: % orders delivered on time
  • Customer Satisfaction: Net Promoter Score tracking
  • Inventory Value: Total stock value by category

2. Sales Reports

Report Name Description Key Metrics
Sales Pipeline Quotations, orders, invoicing status Conversion rate, avg. deal size, pipeline value
Product Performance Best sellers, slow movers Units sold, revenue, profit margin
Customer Analysis Top customers, buying patterns Lifetime value, order frequency, avg. order
Sales Rep Performance Individual sales team metrics Orders won, revenue, commission

3. Production Reports

  • Manufacturing Orders: Active, late, completed orders
  • Work Center Efficiency: Utilization by workstation
  • Production Timeline: Gantt view of scheduled production
  • Material Consumption: Materials used vs. planned
  • Labor Efficiency: Actual vs. standard time per operation
  • Scrap/Waste Report: Waste by product type and material

4. Inventory Reports

  • Stock Levels: Current stock by product/location
  • Reorder Alerts: Products below min. stock level
  • Stock Movement: In/out transactions over time
  • Inventory Turnover: How fast materials/products move
  • Aging Analysis: Slow-moving and obsolete stock
  • Stock Valuation: Total inventory value by category

5. Financial Reports

  • Profitability by Product: Margin analysis per SKU
  • Cost Breakdown: Materials, labor, overhead trends
  • Accounts Receivable: Outstanding invoices by age
  • Purchase Analysis: Supplier spending, payment terms
  • Cash Flow Forecast: Expected revenue vs. expenses

🔧 Technical Features

Report Builder

  • Custom Reports: Drag-and-drop report designer
  • Filters: Date range, product, customer, location
  • Grouping: By product type, series, customer segment
  • Sorting: Ascending/descending by any metric
  • Export: PDF, Excel, CSV formats

Scheduled Reports

  • Email Delivery: Auto-send reports daily/weekly/monthly
  • Recipients: Send to multiple email addresses
  • Conditional Alerts: Send only if KPI thresholds met
  • Format Options: Choose PDF or Excel per recipient

Real-Time Updates

💡 Live Data: All dashboards update in real-time as sales orders, manufacturing orders, and inventory movements occur.

🔗 Dependencies

Module Why Required
vpa_furniture_studio (BASE) Product and dimension data
sale_management (Odoo) Sales order data
web (Odoo) Dashboard UI components

Optional Integrations

  • vpa_furniture_costing: Include cost and profitability data
  • vpa_furniture_manufacturing: Production efficiency reports
  • vpa_furniture_analytics: Advanced analytics and predictions
  • vpa_quality_control: Quality metrics in dashboards

💼 Business Value

  • Make data-driven decisions with real-time KPI visibility
  • Identify top-performing products and customers
  • Spot production bottlenecks before they impact delivery
  • Track profitability trends to adjust pricing strategies
  • Reduce inventory carrying costs through turnover analysis
  • Improve on-time delivery rates with proactive monitoring
📊 ROI: Companies with real-time reporting typically see 20-30% improvement in decision-making speed and 10-15% reduction in operational costs.
