Customer Portal with Order Tracking & Online Configurator

Overview

vpa_furniture_portal provides a customer self-service portal with order tracking, online configurator integration, quotation requests, delivery scheduling, and 3D/AR visualization.

Multi-Tier Portal Access: Standard retail customers, wholesale buyers, and contractors each have role-based access with increasing levels of detail, pricing visibility, and specification access.

Why This Matters: Self-service portals reduce customer service calls by 70%, enable 24/7 ordering, and improve customer satisfaction scores by 40%. Wholesale and contractor portals enable B2B self-service with technical specifications and volume pricing.

Portal Access Tiers

1. Retail Customer Portal

  • Order tracking with production status
  • Basic product configurator access
  • Retail pricing only
  • Quotation requests
  • Delivery scheduling
  • Invoices and delivery notes
  • 3D product preview

2. Wholesale Customer Portal

  • All retail features +
  • Custom discount rates: Individual discount % assigned per customer (e.g., 15%, 20%, 25%)
  • Volume/tiered pricing visibility
  • Bulk order templates
  • Product material specifications
  • Assembly instructions (PDF)
  • Product dimensions and weight details
  • Lead time estimates by quantity
  • Account manager contact info
  • Order history with reorder functionality
  • Optional: Real-time stock visibility (if enabled for specific customers)

3. Contractor Portal

  • All wholesale features +
  • Individual discount agreements: Custom discount rates negotiated per contractor
  • Detailed technical specifications
  • CAD/DXF file downloads (2D drawings)
  • Material cut lists and BOMs
  • Hardware specifications (hinges, slides, handles)
  • Installation guides and videos
  • Load capacity and structural specs
  • Finish/coating details (UV rating, fire rating, etc.)
  • Project-based quoting (multi-room projects)
  • Custom modification requests
  • Direct chat with engineering team
  • Optional: Live inventory visibility (on-hand qty, incoming stock, warehouse locations)
  • Optional: Production schedule access (see estimated completion dates)

Individual Customer Privilege Management

Each wholesale/contractor customer can be configured with custom settings on their contact record:

  • Custom Discount Rate: Individual discount percentage (e.g., Customer A = 15%, Customer B = 22%)
  • Stock Visibility Toggle: Enable/disable real-time inventory viewing
  • Production Schedule Access: Grant visibility into manufacturing timelines
  • CAD Download Permission: Allow/deny technical drawing downloads
  • BOM Visibility: Show/hide bill of materials and cut lists
  • Pricing Visibility: Choose between list price, negotiated price, or volume tiers
  • Document Library Access: Grant access to installation manuals, specs, certifications
  • Credit Limit Display: Show available credit and payment terms
  • Warehouse Location Details: Display which warehouse has stock (multi-location businesses)
  • Direct Chat Access: Enable/disable messaging with internal teams

All privilege settings are managed via checkboxes and fields on the res.partner (customer) form, with default templates for Retail/Wholesale/Contractor that can be customized per individual.

Pricing & Discount Engine

  • Pricelist Assignment: Each customer assigned to specific pricelist (Retail, Wholesale Tier 1-3, Custom Contractor)
  • Customer-Specific Discount: Fixed percentage discount stored on customer record (overrides pricelist)
  • Volume Discounts: Automatic quantity-based discounts (e.g., 10+ units = 5% off, 50+ = 12% off)
  • Contract Pricing: Time-based negotiated rates (e.g., "Customer X gets 18% off until Dec 2025")
  • Product-Specific Discounts: Special pricing on selected SKUs per customer
  • Payment Terms Discounts: Early payment incentives (2/10 Net 30)
  • Price Calculation Priority: Contract price → Customer discount → Volume discount → Pricelist
  • Portal Display: Show "Your Price" vs "List Price" with savings highlighted

Stock & Inventory Visibility Control

When Enabled for Customer:

  • On-Hand Quantity: Real-time available stock per product
  • Incoming Stock: Pending deliveries from suppliers with ETA dates
  • Reserved Stock: Show qty reserved for other orders (optional)
  • Warehouse Locations: Multi-location businesses can show stock per warehouse
  • Lead Time Estimates: "In Stock - Ships Today" vs "Made to Order - 14 days"
  • Low Stock Warnings: Alert when inventory is below threshold
  • Backorder Visibility: Show if item is on backorder with expected date
  • Historical Availability: 30-day stock trend chart (for contractors planning projects)

Security:

  • Stock visibility controlled via portal_stock_visibility boolean field on res.partner
  • Record rules filter stock.quant records based on customer permission
  • Audit log captures all stock queries with timestamp and customer ID

Security & Audit Trail

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Security groups for Retail, Wholesale, Contractor with granular permissions
  • Document Security Rules: Customers can only view their own orders, quotes, and documents
  • Price Visibility Control: Pricing tiers hidden based on customer type
  • Audit Logging: All portal actions logged (logins, downloads, quote requests, order placements)
  • File Access Tracking: CAD/spec file downloads logged with timestamp and user
  • Stock Query Logging: Track all inventory lookups with customer ID and timestamp
  • Discount Change History: Log all modifications to customer discount rates with approval workflow
  • API Rate Limiting: Prevent abuse and unauthorized data scraping
  • Session Management: Secure sessions with auto-timeout and IP tracking
  • Change History: Track all modifications to customer profiles, pricing agreements, access levels
  • Approval Workflows: Wholesale/contractor access requires admin approval
  • Privilege Escalation Alerts: Email notification when customer privileges are upgraded
