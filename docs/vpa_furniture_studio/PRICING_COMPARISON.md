## 💡 Which Payment Option is Best?

### 🏆 Best for Startups: **Monthly Subscription**
- ✅ Lowest upfront cost ($500-$3,500/month)
- ✅ Cancel anytime flexibility
- ✅ Try before committing long-term
- ❌ Highest total cost over 3 years

### 🥇 Best for Committed Users: **Yearly Subscription**
- ✅ Save 17% vs. monthly (2 months free)
- ✅ Guaranteed price for 12 months
- ✅ Better support SLAs
- ✅ Training and webinars included
- ✅ Lower total cost than monthly

### 💎 Best for Long-Term: **One-Time Purchase**
- ✅ Own it forever (perpetual license)
- ✅ Lowest total cost if using 3+ years
- ✅ No vendor lock-in
- ✅ Full customization rights
- ❌ Requires capital budget upfront

## 🔄 Migration & Upgrade Paths

### From Monthly to Yearly
- Switch anytime
- Unused monthly payments credited to annual
- Lock in current pricing

### From Subscription to One-Time
- Apply up to **12 months** of subscription payments toward purchase
- Example: Paid $22K for Advanced yearly → Pay $30K more to own ($52K total)
- Great for validating before committing capital

### Package Upgrades
- Pay difference only
- Pro-rated for subscription customers
- Example: Starter → Advanced = Pay $40K (one-time) or $17K/year difference

### Package Downgrades
- Credit applied to future months (subscription only)
- No refunds on one-time purchases
- 30-day notice required

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I mix one-time and subscription modules?
**Yes!** Buy some modules one-time and subscribe to others. Example: Buy BASE one-time ($2K) + subscribe to Configurator monthly ($250/mo).

### What happens if I stop paying subscription?
Software stops working 30 days after last payment. Your data remains accessible (read-only) for 90 days for export.

### Can I upgrade/downgrade packages?
**Yes!** Upgrade anytime (pay difference). Downgrade on renewal date (credit applied to future).

### Do I get updates with one-time purchase?
**Yes** for first year. After that, purchase annual support contract (20% of license price) for continued updates.

### What's included in support?
- **Starter/Pro:** Email support (1-2 business days)
- **Advanced:** Email + phone (4-hour response)
- **Enterprise/Ultimate:** 24/7 priority support (1-2 hour response)

### Can I pay quarterly instead of yearly?
**Yes!** Quarterly billing available (5% premium over yearly price). Example: Advanced quarterly = $5,775/quarter vs. $22K/year.

### Do you offer educational/non-profit discounts?
**Yes!** 25% discount for educational institutions and registered non-profits. Contact sales for verification.

### What if I need custom features?
All packages include customization hours (Advanced: 8hrs/yr, Enterprise: 20hrs/yr, Ultimate: 40hrs/yr). Additional hours: $150/hr.
