VPA Effective Date Change

Professional Stock Transfer Date Management for Odoo 19

Change effective dates of validated stock transfers with automatic accounting synchronization

Overview

VPA Effective Date Change is a professional solution that allows you to change the effective date of validated stock transfers with automatic synchronization of all related accounting entries and inventory valuations.

Key Features

Pre-Validation Dating

Set custom effective dates before validating stock transfers. Perfect for backdating receipts or deliveries to their actual date.

Post-Validation Wizard

Use the smart wizard to change dates of already validated transfers with one click. No manual journal adjustments needed.

Multi-Currency Support

Automatically recalculates product valuations for foreign currency purchases using the correct exchange rate for the new date.

Access Control

Permission-based security ensures only authorized users can modify transfer dates. Maintains proper audit trails.

Automatic Synchronization

Updates all related records: stock moves, move lines, journal entries, account moves, and valuation layers automatically.

Smart Validation

Validates exchange rate availability for multi-currency transactions and checks database integrity before making changes.

How It Works

Method 1: Before Validation

Step 1: Open any stock picking (delivery order, receipt, or internal transfer)

Step 2: Fill in the "Effective Date" field with your desired date

Step 3: Click "Validate" - the module will use your custom date instead of the current date

Method 2: After Validation

Step 1: Open a stock picking that has already been validated

Step 2: Click the "Change Effective Date" button

Step 3: Select your desired effective date in the wizard popup

Step 4: Confirm - all related records update automatically

What Gets Updated

When you change an effective date, the module automatically synchronizes:

Stock Records

  • Stock Picking (date_done)
  • Stock Moves (date)
  • Stock Move Lines (date)

Accounting

  • Journal Entries (date)
  • Journal Items (date)
  • Account Reconciliation

Valuation

  • Stock Valuation Layers
  • Product Costs (FIFO/Average)
  • Foreign Currency Rates

Important Security Note

This module includes permission-based access control through Odoo security groups. Only users with the appropriate security permissions can change effective dates. This ensures proper audit trails and prevents unauthorized modifications to financial records. The module also validates that exchange rates exist for the selected date before allowing multi-currency valuation updates.
