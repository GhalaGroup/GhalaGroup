Overview

VPA Effective Date Change is a professional solution that allows you to change the effective date of validated stock transfers with automatic synchronization of all related accounting entries and inventory valuations. Perfect for correcting backdated transactions, maintaining accurate financial period reporting, and ensuring audit compliance.

Key Features

Pre-Validation Dating
Set custom effective dates before validating stock transfers. Perfect for backdating receipts or deliveries to their actual date. 		Post-Validation Wizard
Use the smart wizard to change dates of already validated transfers with one click. No manual journal adjustments needed!
Multi-Currency Support
Automatically recalculates product valuations for foreign currency purchases using the correct exchange rate for the new date. 		Access Control
Permission-based security ensures only authorized users can modify transfer dates. Maintains proper audit trails.
Automatic Synchronization
Updates all related records: stock moves, move lines, journal entries, account moves, and valuation layers automatically. 		Smart Validation
Validates exchange rate availability for multi-currency transactions and checks database integrity before making changes.

What Gets Updated

When you change an effective date, the module automatically synchronizes:

Stock Records
  • Stock Picking (date_done)
  • Stock Moves (date)
  • Stock Move Lines (date)
 Accounting
  • Journal Entries (date)
  • Journal Items (date)
  • Account Reconciliation
 Valuation
  • Stock Valuation Layers
  • Product Costs (FIFO/Average)
  • Foreign Currency Rates

How It Works

Method 1: Before Validation
  1. Open any stock picking (delivery, receipt, internal transfer)
  2. Fill in the "Effective Date" field with your desired date
  3. Click "Validate" - uses your custom date instead of current date
 Method 2: After Validation
  1. Open a validated stock picking
  2. Click the "Change Effective Date" button
  3. Select your desired date in the wizard
  4. Confirm - all records update automatically
