Overview

VPA Effective Date Change is a professional solution that allows you to modify effective dates of validated stock transfers with automatic synchronization of all related accounting and inventory records. Handle backdated transactions, multi-currency purchases, and maintain audit compliance. Available for multiple Odoo versions.

How It Works

Get started in 3 simple steps

1

Install

Search: Find "VPA Effective Date Change" in Apps menu

Click Install: Module installs in seconds

Ready: Navigate to Inventory → Operations → Transfers

2

Change Date

Open Transfer: Select validated stock transfer

Click Button: "Change Effective Date" button appears

Select Date: Choose new effective date in wizard

Validate: System checks currency rates (if applicable)

3

Synchronized!

Auto Sync: All records updated instantly

No Restart: Changes apply immediately

View Result: Check stock moves, journal entries, and valuations!
