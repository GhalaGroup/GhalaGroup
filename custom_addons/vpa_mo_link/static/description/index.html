📋 Overview

VPA Manufacturing Order Link revolutionizes how you manage Manufacturing Orders in Odoo. This powerful module extends the standard manufacturing integration to provide flexible workflows that adapt to your business needs - whether you need automatic MO creation, manual linking, or support for products without Bills of Materials.

Perfect for manufacturing businesses that require full control over their production process, support pre-production scenarios, or work with custom/engineered products where BOMs are defined after order confirmation.

Absolutely! The Production route works alongside the standard Manufacture route. You can use both on different products as needed.

Existing MOs are not affected. The module only changes behavior for newly created MOs after installation.
