Overview

VPA Production Commission automates the calculation and management of employee commissions based on Manufacturing Order completion. The system tracks commissionable raw materials consumed during production, applies configurable commission rates per employee, and provides a complete approval workflow from pending to paid status with historical reference tracking.

Key Features

Per-Employee Schemes
Create individual commission schemes for each employee with customizable rates, minimum guarantees, and MO filters! 		MO-Based Commission
Automatic commission calculation when Manufacturing Orders are completed, based on consumed raw materials!
Commission History
View and use historical base amounts from previous MOs for same product. Compare and reference past calculations! 		Commissionable Products

Flexible MO Filtering
Configure which MOs apply per scheme: all MOs, specific workcenters, product categories, or specific products! 		Minimum Guarantee
