Overview

VPA Production Commission automates the calculation and management of employee commissions based on Manufacturing Order completion. The system tracks commissionable raw materials consumed during production, applies configurable commission rates per employee, and provides a complete approval workflow from pending to paid status with historical reference tracking.

Version History

Version 19.0.2.3.1 (Latest)

Cancelled MOs are automatically Not Applicable (August 2026)

  • Automatic cancellation: cancelling a Manufacturing Order cancels its pending commission lines (confirmed/paid lines stay - manager decision)
  • Not Applicable by itself: the MO's commission status computes to Not Applicable for cancelled MOs
  • Generate Commission refused on cancelled MOs
  • Migration applies the same to MOs cancelled before the upgrade

Version 19.0.2.3.0

On-account allocation workflow, Total to Pay card, hardening (August 2026)

  • Not allocated block on year cards whenever the employee has on-account money not applied to any year, with a one-click Allocate action
  • Apply Payment wizard rewritten as a reconciliation-style multi-select table: tick payments to apply, prefilled per-row amounts, live totals, strict validation
  • Card redesign: Total to Pay headline (max of guarantee and total earned), Min. year commission with USD reference, Already Paid, Still to Pay; earned and payment bars with above-minimum and overpaid segments; settled years swap Pay for Close Year
  • Allocated To / On Account columns in the Commission Payments list
  • Payment form: commission fields appear only on commission payments, never on ordinary vendor payments
  • True-up bills numbered per run (#2, #3...) - stable under translation and cancellations
  • Hardening: closed-year allocation guard, reset respects the year lock, currency-rounded card totals, batched Commission Centre computes

Version 19.0.2.2.0

Cleared vs issued payments, symmetric close/reopen (August 2026)

  • Pending reconciliation shown under Already Paid, with a split progress bar (reconciled vs issued awaiting bank)
  • New fields Paid (Cleared) / Issued Not Cleared on the commission year
  • Symmetric close/reopen: closing a settled year stamps the lines it flips to paid; reopening reverts exactly those lines to confirmed
